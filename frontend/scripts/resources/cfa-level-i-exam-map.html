High priority: the five heaviest topics. Weights: CFA Institute 2027 topic outline.

The five gold topics are about 55–70% of the exam. Ethics, Quant, FSA, Equities and Fixed Income decide most results. Master them first, then build the rest around them.

Exam windows: Level I runs in February, May, August and November. Check dates and fees at cfainstitute.org.

12-week route

A plan that follows how topics build on each other

About 25 hours a week. Studying 15 hours a week? Stretch the same order over 20 weeks.

Wk 1–2Quantitative Methods + Economics
The toolkit: time value, statistics, rates.
Wk 3–4Financial Statement Analysis
Biggest reading load. Start early.
Wk 5Corporate Finance
Short; builds on FSA.
Wk 6–7Equities
Valuation models and market structure.
Wk 8–9Fixed Income
Pricing, yield, duration. Practise daily.
Wk 10Derivatives + Alternative Investments
Payoffs and how each asset behaves.
Wk 11Portfolio Construction + Ethics
Ethics last, so it is fresh on exam day.
Wk 12Mock exams + review
Two full mocks. Fix the error pattern, not just the question.

Exam-day rules worth knowing

  • About 90 seconds per question. Flag anything over two minutes and come back.
  • No penalty for wrong answers. Never leave a question blank.
  • Formulas are not given. Know them well enough to rebuild them, not just recite them.
  • Mocks under exam timing in the last two weeks show your real pace.
